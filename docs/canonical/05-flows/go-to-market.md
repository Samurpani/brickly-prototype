# Flow: Go-to-Market (per development)

**[Post-MVP as product; concierge-adjacent in v1]** — Source: `source/bricly-flow-go-to-market.md` (6-phase ideal flow where Bricly orchestrates a development's entire GTM).

## v1 reality

Founding members **run their own GTM**; Bricly's v1 contribution is the Launch Package assets (`launch-package.md`), the hosted lead form (capture Tier 1), and concierge CSV imports of leads from members' ad funnels. The Marketing workspace UI exists in the prototype (Overview / Campaigns / Leads & attribution / Budget / Connections) but is demo-ware; the Marketing Lead role and campaign management arrive **[v1.1]**, ad-platform connections and spend sync **[v1.2]**.

## The 6 ideal phases (for orientation)

1. **Positioning & brand** — v1: Launch Package consultation [MVP·concierge]
2. **Asset production** — v1: Launch Package production [MVP·concierge]
3. **Channel setup** (ads, portals, site, socials) — member-run in v1; Connections tab [v1.2]
4. **Campaign launch & lead flow** — v1: hosted form + CSV import [MVP]; webhooks [v1.2]
5. **Optimisation loop** (attribution → creative iteration → budget shifts) — [v1.1+] Marketing workspace; "Price-Drop Announcement w/ Meta retarget" automation template shows the v1.2 shape
6. **Sell-through management** (pacing, price ladder, re-launch triggers) — [Post-MVP]; see `relaunch-pivot.md`

## What v1 must not pretend

No attribution beyond the source field, no spend data, no campaign metrics — the prototype's Marketing numbers are seeded demo data. Reports' marketing KPIs in v1 show only what the pipeline actually knows (lead counts by source, conversion by source).
