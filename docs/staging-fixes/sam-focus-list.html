Note on the WhatsApp Business API: it's Meta's official way for a business to send WhatsApp messages from software, not from someone's phone. Each account connects its own business number, usually through a provider such as Twilio or 360dialog. Bricly then sends from that number. Two rules come with it:

  • Inside 24 hours of the buyer's last message, any text can be sent.
  • After that, only pre-approved message templates can be sent.

The rep still approves every draft, as today.
