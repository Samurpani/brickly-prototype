1. Purpose

This document tells developers how to build the Create deal flow.

The prototype has one Create deal flow. All entry points use this flow. The flow uses the same records as the deal page, the approvals queue and the payouts.

Before this update, the Deals page used an old dialog. That dialog did not record splits, referrals, funding or dates. It did not use the approvals queue.

NOTEKeep the number of questions low. The flow asks only for the data that makes a deal live. The user adds all other data on the deal page later.

5. Procedure 1 · Create a deal from an existing opportunity

Use this procedure when the opportunity is in Bricly. In this example, an owner creates the deal. A second agent shares the deal.

5.1 Buyer step

  1. On the Deals page, click + Add deal.Result: The drawer opens on the Buyer step (Figure 3).
  2. Type a buyer name, an opportunity ID, a development or a unit in the search field.Result: The list shows matching open opportunities (Figure 4). The latest stage is at the top.
  3. Click the opportunity.Result: The Sale step opens. Data from the opportunity fills the fields.
NOTEThe Buyer step has no Next button in this mode. A click on an opportunity moves to the next step. A rep sees only the rep's own opportunities.

5.2 Sale step

  1. Make sure that the unit is correct.If the opportunity has more than one unit, select the unit. The flow disables units that other buyers hold.
  2. If necessary, add co-buyers.
  3. If the development has finish packs or layouts, select them.Result: The price box adds the cost of the options.
  4. Make sure that the final agreed price is correct.The hint shows the difference from the list price. A price below the floor price gets a flag.
  5. Enter the POS signed date and the deposit paid.The deposit starts at 10% of the price.
  6. Select the funding: Cash, Bank loan or Mixed.
  7. Optional: enter the target final deed date.
  8. Optional: for Bank loan or Mixed, enter the loan approval deadline.
  9. Select Upload now or Add later for the signed POS document.
  10. Click Next.

5.3 Commission step

  1. Keep Default · 2%, or select Custom.For Custom, enter a rate or a fixed amount. Enter a reason.
  2. If an agent shares the deal or a contact referred the buyer, set the Referral or shared deal toggle to on (Figure 8).
  3. Search for the agent, the contact or the team member. Click the result.
  4. If the person is not in Contacts, click Add “name” as a new contact (Figure 9).
  5. For a new contact, select Agent or agency or Contact. Enter the name. The phone and the agency are optional.
  6. Click Add & link.Result: Bricly adds the contact to Contacts. The flow links the contact (Figure 10).
  7. Enter their share as a percentage of the commission or as a fixed amount.
  8. If a team member shares the deal, set the Split with a team member toggle to on. Select the person and the percentages (Figure 11).
  9. Read the breakdown. Make sure that “We keep” is correct.
  10. Click Next.
NOTEIf the buyer's contact already has a “referred by” value, the toggle starts on and the referrer is linked.

5.4 Review step

  1. Read the summary. Make sure that the data is correct.
  2. Read the list “Still needed on the deal”. The deal page tracks these items. They do not stop the deal.
  3. Optional: type a note for the deal team.
  4. Click Create deal.Result: Bricly makes the deal and opens the deal page (Figure 13). The unit changes to Reserved.
NOTEThe button shows Submit for approval when the deal needs manager approval. Refer to 9.2.

6. Procedure 2 · Record a sale outside Bricly

Use this procedure when the buyer has no opportunity in Bricly. In this example, a rep records the sale.

  1. On the Deals page, click + Add deal.
  2. Click No opportunity in Bricly? (Figure 15).
  3. Search for the buyer. If the buyer is not in Contacts, click Add “name” as a new contact (Figure 16).
  4. Enter the name. The phone is optional. Click Add & link.
  5. Select the development and the unit (Figure 17).The list disables units that are on hold or reserved.
  6. Click Next. Do the Sale and Commission steps. Refer to 5.2 and 5.3.
  7. On the Review step, click Submit for approval (Figure 18).Result: The deal shows “Awaiting approval” (Figure 19). The approvals queue gets a “Deal from POS” request (Figure 20).
CAUTIONThe flow makes an external opportunity when you click Next on the Buyer step. If you close the drawer before the deal is made, the flow deletes this opportunity. It also deletes a contact that it added. Keep this cleanup in the build.
  1. The manager clicks Approve.Result: The deal changes to Active. The unit changes to Reserved. Bricly declines other hold requests on the unit.
  2. Or, the manager clicks Reject and gives a reason.Result: Bricly deletes the deal. The opportunity goes back to its last stage. The unit does not change.

7. Procedure 3 · Convert an opportunity at an early stage

Use this procedure when the buyer signed the POS outside Bricly, and the opportunity is at an early stage.

  1. Open the opportunity.
  2. Click Actions. Click Convert to deal (Figure 2).Result: The Sale step opens. A message shows the stages that the flow will skip (Figure 21).
  3. Do the Sale, Commission and Review steps. Refer to 5.2 to 5.4.
NOTEThe flow marks each skipped stage as “Skipped — POS recorded” in the opportunity activity. If the unit is not reserved for this buyer, a rep must get manager approval. A manager's deal goes live and Bricly logs it.

8. Procedure 4 · Add a referral after the deal exists

  1. Open the deal.
  2. On the Sales team & commission card, click Edit team & split.
  3. Click + Add referral.Result: A new row shows with the role Referrer and 10%. The deal owner's share decreases by 10%.
  4. Select a person. If the person is not in Contacts, select + New contact… (Figure 22).
  5. Enter the name. The phone is optional. Click Add (Figure 23).
  6. Make sure that the shares total 100%.
  7. Type a reason. Click Save split.

9. Rules

9.1 Commission calculation

total     = price × rate ÷ 100   (or the fixed amount)
referral  = total × referral % ÷ 100   (or the fixed amount)
pool      = total − referral
member    = pool × member % ÷ 100
we keep   = pool   (when the referrer is outside the team)
we keep   = total  (when the referrer is a team member)

The deal record stores each share as a percentage of the total. The referral share is first. The team shares use the remaining percentage. Rounding goes to the deal owner.

9.2 Approval

ApprovalWhen it is necessaryEffect until approval
Deal (“Deal from POS”)The user is a rep, and the unit is not reserved for this buyer. This includes all sales outside Bricly from a rep.The deal shows “Awaiting approval”. The unit does not change.
Commission splitThe user is a rep, and the split is different from the default. Differences are: rate not 2%, more than one person, an external person, or a deal owner below 100%.The deal is live. The commission shows “Awaiting approval”.
NOTEA manager does not need approval. Bricly logs the manager's decision on the deal.

The flow adds a flag for review when the price is below the floor price, the commission is custom, or a layout needs developer sign-off. Flags do not stop the deal.
