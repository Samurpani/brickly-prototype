The mental model

Where Bricly sits in the developer's journey

Land, permits, pricing and drawings happen before us (future verticals, greyed). Bricly starts at go-to-market and runs five stages. The CRM is the system of record; Studio generates the media; Present is the agent-facing way to sell with it.
